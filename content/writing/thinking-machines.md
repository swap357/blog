> “But that only permitted other men with machines to enslave them.”

— Frank Herbert, *Dune* (1965). [Quote source](https://publications.lawschool.cornell.edu/jlpp/2023/10/19/in-the-likeness-of-the-human-mind-ai-liability-and-the-speculative-fiction-of-dune/).

**Paraphrase:**

People entrusted their reasoning to machines, expecting greater freedom.\
Their dependence gave those who controlled the machines power over them.\
Domination continued through the very tools that were supposed to make them independent.
